How it works

From last year's numbers
to next week's plan.

    Drop in your guest counts

  2. 02

    TableCast learns your rhythm

    Weekdays, seasons, weather, holidays, school breaks, paydays and closing days. Then it tests itself on recent weeks it has never seen.

  3. 03

    Plan the week in a minute

    Guests per day with a realistic range, staff per shift, prep quantities and revenue, as a dashboard, PDF, calendar and weekly email.
